# OKX convert: how to swap crypto, understand the quoted rate, and choose between Convert and spot trading

Searching for **OKX convert** usually means you want to swap one cryptocurrency for another without opening a trading chart, choosing an order type, or waiting for an order to fill. OKX Convert is designed for that use case: select the asset you hold, select the asset you want, review the quote, and confirm.

The simple interface is useful, especially for small balances or straightforward swaps. The important detail is that **“0 fees” does not mean the quote is identical to the spot-market price**. OKX uses a quote-based model for Convert, and the spread is built into the price you see before confirmation. For a small conversion, that convenience may be worth it. For a larger trade, comparing the quote with the spot market can make a meaningful difference.

## What is OKX Convert?

OKX Convert is a crypto-to-crypto conversion tool. It lets you exchange one supported digital asset for another through a quoted transaction instead of placing an order on an order book.

For example, you may be able to convert:

- USDT to BTC
- USDC to ETH
- BTC to ETH
- ETH to a supported stablecoin
- A small leftover token balance into another available asset

The exact routes depend on the assets available to your account at the time of conversion. OKX advises users to check the actual **From** and **To** asset lists inside Convert rather than assuming that every asset with a spot market can always be converted directly. Availability can vary by market, region, account status, and current platform conditions.

Convert is different from an ordinary spot trade in one basic way:

- **Convert:** accept the quoted amount shown in the preview.
- **Spot trading:** place an order against the order book, with maker or taker fees and possible execution differences.

The Convert route is easier to understand at first glance because you do not need to choose between a market order and a limit order. You also do not need to manage an open order. The trade-off is that you have less control over the execution price.

## Does OKX Convert really have no fees?

OKX presents Convert as having no separate transaction-fee line. That means you will not normally see a conventional maker or taker commission deducted after the conversion.

However, the quoted price includes a spread. In other words, the cost is reflected in the conversion rate rather than displayed as a separate fee. OKX’s current spread disclosure lists a maximum spread of **1.00% for all pairs**, while noting that the actual spread can vary by market and region. Market volatility and price movement between the quote and confirmation can also affect whether the order succeeds.

This distinction matters:

> A transaction can show “0 fees” and still return less crypto than a simple spot-price calculation suggests.

Suppose a chart shows that your crypto is worth approximately $1,000. That does not guarantee that Convert will give you exactly $1,000 worth of the destination asset. The conversion quote may include a spread based on the pair, trade size, liquidity, and current market conditions.

The preview screen is therefore the number that matters. Before confirming, compare:

1. The amount you are sending.
2. The quoted exchange rate.
3. The exact amount you will receive.
4. The time remaining before the quote expires.
5. The approximate price on the relevant spot pair.

If the difference is small and convenience is your priority, Convert may be reasonable. If the difference is noticeable, open the spot market and compare the available order-book price and applicable fee.

## How to use OKX Convert

The exact menu names may differ slightly between the website and mobile app, but the process follows the same sequence.

### Step 1: Open Convert

Log in to your OKX account and open the Convert feature. On supported app versions, the path is generally found under **Trade > Convert**. OKX’s own walkthrough describes the process as opening Convert, selecting the assets, entering an amount, reviewing the quote, and confirming the transaction.

### Step 2: Select the asset you are sending

Choose the cryptocurrency you currently hold. The available balance shown in Convert may depend on where your funds are stored.

For some OKX account layouts, Convert uses the balance available in your Trading account. If your funds are sitting in Funding, the conversion screen may show an insufficient balance even though the assets still appear in your overall account. In that case, check whether an internal transfer is required before continuing.

### Step 3: Select the asset you want to receive

Choose the destination cryptocurrency from the available list. If the asset does not appear, it may not currently be supported for that conversion route.

Do not assume that two assets can be converted directly just because both are listed on OKX. The dropdown inside Convert is the practical source of truth for your account at that moment. OKX also notes that BTC and ETH may have more limited direct conversion routes than many other tokens.

### Step 4: Enter the amount

You can usually enter either:

- The amount of the asset you want to send; or
- The amount of the destination asset you want to receive.

Check the denomination carefully. A crypto conversion screen can make a large number look reassuring even when the unit is different from what you intended. This is especially relevant when moving between tokens with very different prices.

### Step 5: Review the quote

The preview shows the estimated or quoted amount you will receive. Review it before clicking the final confirmation button.

OKX states that the conversion rate refreshes every 10 seconds. If the quote expires before you confirm, you may need to refresh and review a new quote.

### Step 6: Confirm within the quote window

If the amount is acceptable, confirm the conversion before the quote expires. After completion, the original asset balance is reduced and the converted asset is credited to your account or Portfolio area.

### Step 7: Check the transaction record

If you want to verify the completed amount, open your order history or Portfolio history and filter for Convert transactions. The order details should show the amount sent, amount received, and other transaction information.

## Is OKX Convert cheaper than spot trading?

There is no universal answer because the comparison depends on the live Convert quote and your spot fee tier.

Convert may look cheaper because no separate fee is displayed. But the spread is already inside the quote. Spot trading displays maker or taker fees separately, while the execution price comes from the order book. A spot trade with a low fee can still produce a worse result if the market is thin or the order is large enough to consume several price levels.

A practical comparison looks like this:

1. Enter the same amount in Convert.
2. Note the destination amount shown in the preview.
3. Open the corresponding spot pair.
4. Check your maker and taker fee rate.
5. Estimate the spot result using the available order-book prices.
6. Compare the final amount, not just the headline fee.

OKX says the fee rates visible when logged out may not match the rates attached to your account. Your actual fee tier should be checked while logged in through the trading-fee section or the order-placement panel.

For a beginner, Convert can be easier to operate correctly. For an experienced trader, spot trading may offer better price control. Neither route is automatically cheaper in every situation.

## Why did I receive less crypto than expected?

There are several common explanations.

### The quote included a spread

The chart price is not necessarily the same as the Convert quote. OKX explains that the quoted price includes a spread influenced by the instrument, market, liquidity, and conditions at the time of the transaction.

### The quote expired

If you confirm after the quote window closes, the platform may require a refreshed quote. The new rate can differ because the market moved.

### The amount was above the current maximum

Convert limits are not necessarily one fixed number for every asset. The maximum can depend on the specific cryptocurrency and the conditions applying to your account. If the amount is too large, the preview may fail or ask you to reduce the amount.

### You split the conversion into several transactions

Splitting a large conversion does not automatically reduce the cost. Each transaction receives a separate quote, so the spread is applied separately to each conversion. Compare the total received across the proposed method before dividing the trade.

### Some funds are committed elsewhere

Your total balance may include assets locked in open orders, Earn products, margin positions, or trading bots. Those funds can appear in your total account value while remaining unavailable for Convert.

## What if the asset I want is not available?

Start by checking the Convert dropdown again and confirm that you selected the correct account and asset. If the token is still missing, the direct route may not be available for your region or account.

In some cases, users may consider a two-step route through a widely quoted asset such as a stablecoin or another major cryptocurrency. For example, the platform may offer Asset A to USDT and USDT to Asset B even when Asset A to Asset B is not available directly.

That approach has an important drawback: each conversion receives its own quote. Two conversions can therefore create two separate spreads and two separate execution points. Use the two-step route only after comparing the combined result with a direct spot-market trade.
